cat /sys/hdmi/lcdbl
Change from 1 to 0 while HDMI is plugged in by default unless changed in settings.
lcdbl stands for LCD Backlight.
it simply kills the power to the physical LCD panel's backlight driver circuit by writing a 0 to this node.
natively issue echo 0 > /sys/hdmi/lcdbl to save battery power and avoid distracting light leakage.
